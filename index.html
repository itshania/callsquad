What services does Call Squad Media provide?+

We specialize in performance-based lead generation, live transfers, inbound and outbound calling, and customer acquisition solutions for the U.S. insurance industry.

Which industries do you serve?+

We primarily work with Medicare, Final Expense, Health Insurance, Home Health Care, and other insurance-related campaigns.

Are your leads TCPA compliant?+

Yes. We prioritize compliance and follow industry best practices. Compliance options such as TrustedForm and Jornaya can be supported when required by the campaign.

Do you offer exclusive leads or shared leads?+

We offer exclusive leads, shared leads, and live transfers depending on your campaign requirements.

What is a live transfer?+

A live transfer connects your licensed agent directly with a qualified consumer in real time, helping improve contact and conversion rates.

Can you customize lead generation campaigns?+

Yes. We build customized campaigns based on your target market, eligibility criteria, geography, and business goals.

What pricing models do you offer?+

We offer flexible pricing models, including CPL (Cost Per Lead), CPA (Cost Per Acquisition), and live transfer pricing based on campaign requirements.

How quickly can we get started?+

Most campaigns can be launched within a few business days after onboarding, compliance review, and campaign setup.

Do you work with agencies, FMOs, and carriers?+

Yes. We partner with insurance agencies, FMOs, carriers, and marketing organizations across the United States.
